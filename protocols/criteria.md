# Eligibility criteria (title and abstract screening, then full text)

The review maps the AI, data, and computing technologies that serve, or could serve, a digital twin of an agrivoltaic system.

## Include a record only if ALL of the following hold

I1. System. The study concerns an agrivoltaic system: photovoltaic (PV) generation co-located with agricultural production on the same land unit. This covers crops or pasture under or between PV arrays (elevated, stilted, interspace, vertical bifacial, tracking), grazing under PV, and PV greenhouses or PV-shaded protected cultivation in which plants are grown. Aquavoltaics (PV over fish ponds), PV on farm buildings with no plants under the PV, and "solar farms" with no agricultural production are NOT agrivoltaic for this review.

I2. Enabler. The study develops or applies at least one of:
  (a) an AI, machine learning, or data-driven method (for example ANN, CNN, LSTM, random forest, SVM, gradient boosting, reinforcement learning, statistical forecasting or regression models fitted to data, surrogate models);
  (b) a digital twin, digital shadow, or a virtual replica explicitly linked to an installation;
  (c) a computational model of agrivoltaic system behavior (light, irradiance or shading under the array; PV energy or electrical yield; crop growth or yield; microclimate; soil water, irrigation, or evapotranspiration) that is used for estimation, prediction, design or operational optimization, or control;
  (d) a sensing, IoT, monitoring, data-acquisition, or computing system (edge, fog, cloud) for agrivoltaic data.

I3. Scale. The model or system represents a specific agrivoltaic installation, design, or generic system configuration at plot, system, or farm scale.

I4. Publication type. Peer-reviewed primary research: journal article or full conference paper. English language. Any publication year up to the search date.

## Exclude if ANY of the following hold

E1. Not agrivoltaic (fails I1), including solar farms without agriculture, aquavoltaics, and generic PV or generic agriculture studies.
E2. No enabler (fails I2): field experiments that only report measured outcomes, agronomic trials without a model or data system, economic-only (LCOE, NPV, business model), life-cycle assessment-only, policy, legal, social acceptance, perception, or landscape studies.
E3. Regional, national, or global potential assessments and land-suitability or site-selection mapping (for example GIS multi-criteria analysis) that do not model a specific system (fails I3).
E4. Secondary studies: reviews, surveys, bibliometric analyses, perspectives, editorials, commentaries. (These are used as related work, not as included studies.)
E5. Not peer-reviewed primary research: preprints, theses, book chapters, conference abstracts or extended abstracts of two pages or less, posters, reports, datasets, errata.
E6. Not in English, or no full record available.

When the title and abstract are insufficient to decide I2 or I3, mark "MAYBE" (it goes to full-text assessment). When the record is clearly agrivoltaic and the abstract mentions modeling, simulation, sensors, forecasting, optimization, or AI, prefer MAYBE over EXCLUDE.
